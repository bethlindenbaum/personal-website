About Me

I am a rising senior at Duke University, double majoring in Electrical & Computer Engineering, and Computer Science. I have technical experience in hardware, firmware, software, machine learning, and nanoelectronics, with proficiency in C/C++, Python, Java, and Verilog. I find great interest in the interworkings of electrics and computers, and love to work at all levels of the technology stack, from semiconductor materials to high-level programming.

I have gained my experience with computer architecture and digital system design from a Pac-Man game I developed on FPGA, where I worked with Verilog, MIPS assembly, and a custom-designed processor. I further honed my hardware development skills during my role as a Computer Vision Intern at Lockheed Martin, where I implemented a deep learning model on a low-power dev board that included an FPGA and a System on Chip. I developed my skills in nanoelectronics and semiconductors from my lab work at Duke, where I helped research and test printed transistors. I sharpened my skills in firmware and embedded system design through my internship as an Embedded Linux Software Engineer at Schneider Electric, where I programmed an automated wireless communication test suite in C and C++ on an ESP32 microcontroller, and implemented wireless communication protocols, such as Bluetooth and Zigbee, in Python.

Outside the classroom, I am very involved on Duke's campus. I am the Vice President of the Jewish Student Union at Duke, which has given me the opportunity to help build a community where Jewish students feel connected and supported. I am also a part of the Women's Club Basketball team, Society of Women Engineers, and Duke Technology Scholars (DTech). Additionally, I like to play pickup basketball, weightlift, spend time outdoors, read, and listen to music.

More information about my projects, experience, and qualifications can be found on my resume, Github, and Linkedin.
